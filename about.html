About

Why this site exists and who runs it.

Every week, things go missing at World University of Bangladesh: phones left in classrooms, ID cards dropped in the cafeteria, bags forgotten on the university bus. Usually someone finds them, but there's no easy way for the finder and the owner to meet.

WUB Lost & Found is one place where students and staff can post what they lost or found, search what others have posted, and get things back to their owners.

Who runs it

It's a student-run project, made by a WUB student for the campus community. It is not an official service of World University of Bangladesh.

Campus

World University of Bangladesh, Avenue 6 & Lake Drive Road, Sector 17/H, Uttara, Dhaka-1230. Official university website: wub.edu.bd.

Contact & reports

To report a fake or inappropriate post, or to suggest an improvement, open an issue on the project's GitHub page: github.com/hadi07810301/hadi07810301-wub-lost-found.

Privacy

  • Posts, including the contact details you enter, are public so the owner or finder can reach you.
  • No login is needed. Your browser keeps a random ID so you can edit or delete your own posts.
  • Please don't post ID numbers, bank card details or other private information.

Tips & FAQ

How to get your things back faster, and how to return things safely.

😟 If you lost something

  • Check the found items first: it may already be posted.
  • Retrace your steps: classroom, library, cafeteria, washroom, university bus.
  • Post it with a clear title, the place and the date. A photo helps a lot.
  • Ask at the reception or the department office.
  • Lost your student ID? Tell your department office so it can't be misused.

🙌 If you found something

  • Post it quickly, while the owner is still looking.
  • Don't share every detail. Keep one thing secret (what's inside, a sticker, the wallpaper) and ask the owner to describe it.
  • For ID cards, cover the ID number in your photo.
  • Valuable items like phones, wallets and laptops can be handed to the university office.
  • Never ask for a reward before returning something.

🤝 When you meet

  • Meet on campus in a busy place, like the library entrance or reception.
  • Go during class hours, and bring a friend if you want.
  • The owner should show proof: describe the item, unlock the phone, or show an ID.

🔒 Stay safe online

  • Your contact info is public on your post. Use a number or account you're comfortable sharing.
  • Never send money or OTP codes to someone who says they found your item.
  • Once the item is back, mark your post as reunited or delete it.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to post?

No. Anyone can post. Your posts are linked to the browser you used, so you can edit or delete them later from My posts on the same device.

How do I mark my item as returned?

Open your post (from My posts) and tap "Mark as reunited". It moves to the Reunited list so people know it's sorted.

Someone says the item is theirs. How do I check?

Ask them to describe something you didn't mention in the post: what's inside, a mark, a sticker, the phone's wallpaper. If they can't, don't hand it over.

Is this the official university lost and found?

No. It's a student-run board to help WUB students and staff. For valuable or official items, you can also contact the university office.

How long do posts stay up?

Until the person who posted marks them reunited or deletes them. Please clean up your posts once your item is back.

I saw a fake or rude post. What do I do?

Use the contact on the About page to report it, and it will be removed.
